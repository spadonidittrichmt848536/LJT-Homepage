I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working in the HKUST NLP Group under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML). Before my Ph.D., I received my B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He.

## Research Interests & Skills
